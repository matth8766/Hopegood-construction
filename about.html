Hopegood Construction Ltd.

Capable across the whole project.

Based in Rocky Mountain House, Hopegood Construction Ltd. works throughout Central Alberta on residential construction, renovations, additions, exterior projects and finishing work.

Our experience across framing, roofing, drywall, painting and finishing gives us a practical understanding of how the pieces of a project come together. That matters whether we're completing one scope or coordinating a larger build.

We believe clients should know what is happening on their project. Clear expectations, communication and work we are comfortable standing behind are central to how we operate.
